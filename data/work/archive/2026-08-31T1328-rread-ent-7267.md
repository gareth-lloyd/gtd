  **What it asks for**

  A configurable onboarding checklist that gives a hotel an explicit to-do list of setup
  actions. Deployed at *hotel* level, configured at *portfolio / association / enterprise*
  level — every hotel in the IHG deployment inherits a predefined checklist.

  - **Required items** cannot be self-checked; the checklist reads real system state
    (e.g. "add an escalation contact" only ticks once one is actually configured).
  - **Recommended items** can be self-ticked regardless of underlying action
    (e.g. "update early check-in / late checkout pricing").
  - Rule-based conditional inclusion per hotel against entitlements / enabled products —
    Mobile Check-In items only if they have Mobile Check-In, etc.
  - Surfacing: persistent button top-right of the portal with count badge + alert
    indicator; opens a modal; configurable rule (not hardcoded) to auto-open on login
    while required items are pending.
  - Every item deep-links into the settings page that completes it.
  - Explicitly **not** Appcues — Appcues can't read product state, inherit portfolio
    config, or evaluate entitlement rules.

  **Why now (per ticket)**: IHG asked for it by name, and it matches an existing internal
  conviction that onboarding best practice should live in the product rather than in CSM
  handholding.

  **Seed items (via Taylor, from IHG)**
  1. Set up escalation contacts. (Required)
  2. Update early check-in / late checkout upsell items — IHG doesn't require the price to
     change, just for the hotel to visit and re-save the page.
  3. A third item was mentioned but not captured — needs recovering from Taylor.
